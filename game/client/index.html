        How to Play

        1. Fire Marbles

        Click or press Space to fire marbles from your cannon. Different marble types create different units:

        • 🔴 Attack Marble - Spawns a strong Melee unit
        • 🟢 Buff Marble - Spawns a supportive Buff unit
        • 🟡 Split Marble - Spawns two weaker units

        2. Claim Territory

        Your units automatically move and attack. Claim edges (walls) by having your units touch them. Claimed walls turn your team color.

        3. Destroy Enemy Spawners

        Send your units to attack the enemy spawner (circle with team number). Destroy all enemy spawners to win!

        4. Use Cards

        Play cards for special effects like healing, damage boosts, or spawning extra units. Cards cost Gold to play.

        5. Manage Your Loadout

        Before each match, select a faction and a loadout of 3 cards. Different factions have unique abilities.
